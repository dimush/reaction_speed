Reaction Speed

## Full description (≤4000 chars)
How fast are your reflexes? Reaction Speed is a simple, focused game that measures how quickly you react to a visual cue — no ads-first gimmicks, no clutter, just a red target and a timer.

HOW IT WORKS
Ten targets appear one after another on the screen, each after a random pause so you can't predict the timing. Tap each target the instant it appears. At the end of the series, your score is calculated from those ten taps: unusually slow taps (statistical outliers, e.g. when you got distracted) are discarded, and the rest are averaged to give your result in milliseconds. Tap too early and it counts as a false start.

COMPETE WORLDWIDE
Sign in with Google Play Games to submit your best average to the world leaderboard and see how you rank against players everywhere. Unlock achievements as you improve your reaction time, build up a streak of series, and chase new personal bests.

TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
The statistics screen keeps a history chart of every series you've played, so you can see your reaction time trend over time, along with your all-time best, your average of the last 10 series, and your top 10 personal results.

DESIGNED TO BE FAIR
Timing is measured precisely from the moment the target is drawn to the moment you tap it, so your score reflects your real reflexes. Optional target and stone sounds and vibration feedback help you stay focused during a series.

Reaction Speed works fully offline for solo play. Signing in with Google Play Games is optional and only needed if you want to appear on the world leaderboard or unlock achievements.
